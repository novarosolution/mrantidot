Full description:
Mr Antidot is a home-services booking platform that connects customers with technicians and helps admins manage day-to-day operations in one place.

For customers
• Browse and book home services easily
• Manage addresses, bookings, and notifications
• Track job progress and view offers
• Confirm on-site work with verified job photos

For technicians
• View assigned jobs and complete work on the go
• Capture camera photos with GPS tagging for job verification
• Manage leave, profile, and job history

For admins
• Manage customers, technicians, and bookings
• Control services, offers, reviews, and notifications
• Monitor operations from a dedicated admin experience

Why Mr Antidot
Trusted booking flow, clear job tracking, and photo + location verification so customers can feel confident that work was done on site.
