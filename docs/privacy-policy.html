Privacy Policy

Mr Antidot · Last updated: 5 November 2026

Mr Antidot (“we”, “our”, or “the App”) is a home-services booking platform. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have when you use our mobile application and related services.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information — name, phone number, email, and role (customer, technician, or admin).
  • Booking & service data — addresses, service requests, schedules, job status, and support messages.
  • Location — with your permission, to autofill service addresses and to tag job verification photos so customers can confirm on-site work.
  • Photos & camera — with your permission, for booking problem images and technician job verification photos.
  • Device & usage — basic technical data needed to run the App (for example app version and crash diagnostics if enabled).

2. How we use information

  • Create and manage your account
  • Process bookings and assign technicians
  • Verify completed jobs with photo and GPS tags
  • Send notifications about bookings, offers, and account activity
  • Improve reliability, security, and customer support
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Permissions

Camera, photos, and location are requested only for the features described above. You can deny or revoke these permissions in your device settings. Some features may not work without them.

4. Sharing

We share information only as needed to operate the service — for example with assigned technicians and admins handling your booking. We do not sell your personal information. We may use trusted infrastructure providers (hosting, database) who process data on our instructions.

5. Data retention & security

We keep account and booking data while your account is active and as needed for operations, disputes, and legal requirements. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information.

6. Children’s privacy

Mr Antidot is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your choices

  • Update profile and address details in the App
  • Control camera / location permissions in device settings
  • Request account deletion or data-related help by contacting us

8. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Mr Antidot support team through the in-app Help section or the email / phone published on your Google Play store listing.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after updates means you accept the revised policy.
